## Scope and result

The complete channel history available through Slack was read, including every thread on Anthony's top-level reports. A paginated author search returned 137 messages/replies and the channel read returned 72 top-level messages; these reduce to 47 substantive findings below. Messages from Anthony Walcott were audit reminders, not additional findings.

- Findings 1-41 apply wholly or partly to this FireBAM checkout. No currently reproducible local regression remains. Finding 7 was resolved in-thread as a return-value contract misunderstanding, and the Jito-Solana half of finding 5 is not present here.
- This recheck closed two trigger-fidelity gaps: repeated BAM `seq_id` now has the original five-member-then-singleton dedup case, and the D12 two-member nonce rejection now passes through real pack and wire serialization.
- All three zip archives and six attached PoC diffs were read. D18, D26, D36, D12, D45, D46, D56, and the CU differential now have an exact or behaviorally equivalent regression identified below.
- Findings 42-47 are reports against Jito-Solana 4.2/master components that are absent or disabled here. They are logged as structurally not applicable, not claimed as verified in Jito-Solana.

Status vocabulary:

- **PASS**: the vulnerable behavior is absent from the current checkout and relevant tests pass.
- **PASS after audit fix**: a residual issue was found, fixed, and covered during this audit.
- **RESOLVED / not a defect**: the report's premise was corrected in the Slack thread and the current contract was verified.
- **N/A to checkout**: the named Jito-Solana/BAM-client execution path does not exist in this repository state.

## Test-suite comment traceability

Regression comments describe the original bad behavior and protected scenario without naming a reporter or embedding Slack links. Provenance stays in this audit log. Findings against absent Jito-only components are documented here rather than represented by misleading local tests.

## Audit-case uniqueness

The 47 findings map to 41 primary local test locations or N/A determinations. Shared tests are used only when the same scenario protects multiple reports: 1/8 (authentication), 6/31 (CU reporting), 13/43 (BAM override), and topology-related findings.
